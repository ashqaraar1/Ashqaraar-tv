Privacy Policy

App: Ashqaraar TV
Last updated: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ashqaraar TV ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information. The app may collect the following non-personal data automatically:

  • Advertising ID: Used to serve relevant advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Device information: Collected by third-party SDKs (Google AdMob, Firebase) for analytics and crash reporting purposes.
  • Usage data: Aggregated, anonymized data about how features are used, collected by Firebase Analytics.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To send push notifications about new content and live events (only if you grant notification permission)
  • To improve app performance and fix crashes via Firebase
  • To load and display content from our servers

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – advertising
  • Google Firebase – push notifications, analytics, remote configuration

4. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads in the app. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to show personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

5. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet: Required to stream video content and load data.
  • Notifications: To send alerts about new content and live matches (optional).
  • Network State: To check internet connectivity before loading content.
  • Wake Lock: To keep the screen on while a video is playing.
  • Draw Over Other Apps: To display a floating mini video player while multitasking. Only activated when you tap the mini player button.

6. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Any data collected by third-party services (Google, Firebase) is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Ashqaraar TV is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
